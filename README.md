# Bill & Contract Tracker

**Sözleşme ve Fatura Takip** — an Android organiser for bills, contracts and warranty documents, with English and Turkish interfaces.

[Get the Android app on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sozlesmetakip.app) · [Website and guides](https://belgekasam.site/) · [Product Hunt launch](https://www.producthunt.com/products/sozlesme-ve-fatura-takip)

## What it helps you do

- Scan documents or add records manually; check recognised text before saving.
- Review the recurring monthly expenses you record.
- Track contract and warranty dates with reminders.
- Export and restore your own backup files, with optional password protection.

The free version includes ads and a document limit. Optional one-time PRO unlocks unlimited documents and removes ads. Current pricing is shown in the app through Google Play. No bank connection or automatic bill payment is provided. Reminder delivery depends on notification permissions and device settings.
